## What goes here

Business and product documentation for the **Digital Sales Room (DSR) for Events** concept — the direction BENE is moving toward after the pivot discussions of August 2026.

The DSR concept in one sentence: an interactive client collaboration workspace where event agencies turn their venue knowledge into a shareable, two-sided pitch board that ends with a mutually signed-off spec.

## Relationship to Personal Venue Catalog

The **Personal Venue Catalog** ([../Personal_Venue_Catalog/](../Personal_Venue_Catalog/)) is not discarded — it is the data layer inside the DSR. Venues still need to be ingested, structured, and searchable. The pivot is about what the primary **output** is: not search results for the internal team, but an interactive pitch delivered to the client.

```
Personal Venue Catalog  →  data layer (ingestion, ETL, metadata, search)
Digital Sales Room      →  output layer (pitch board, client portal, snapshot)
```
