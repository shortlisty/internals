# Digital Sales Room for Events

> **Audience:** Founders, team.
> **Purpose:** Concept overview and document index for the Digital Sales Room (DSR) positioning. Explains the relationship between the catalog data layer and the pitch board output layer, and where to find each document.

---

## What goes here

Business and product documentation for the **Digital Sales Room (DSR) for Events** concept — the direction Shortlisty is moving toward after the alignment discussions of July 2026.

The DSR concept in one sentence: an interactive client collaboration workspace where event agencies turn their venue knowledge into a shareable, two-sided pitch board that ends with a mutually signed-off spec.

## Relationship to Personal Venue Catalog

The **Personal Venue Catalog** ([../personal-venue-catalog/](../personal-venue-catalog/)) is not discarded — it is the data layer inside the DSR. Venues still need to be ingested, structured, and searchable. The position shift is about what the primary **output** is: not search results for the internal team, but an interactive pitch delivered to the client.

```
Personal Venue Catalog  →  data layer (ingestion, ETL, metadata, search)
Digital Sales Room      →  output layer (pitch board, client portal, snapshot)
```
