# What is OiQb?

> **Audience:** Anyone.
> **Purpose:** Plain-language product overview — what OiQb is, the problem it solves, and how it makes money. No technical background assumed.

---

## The problem

An event agency receives a client brief. The planner knows which venues would work — they have seen them, worked with them, collected the files. But those files are scattered: a PDF in someone's email from two years ago, a floor plan on a shared drive, a price list in a WhatsApp thread.

Assembling that into something the client can review takes hours. What gets sent is usually a static PDF. Feedback arrives across email and WhatsApp. Nobody has a clear record of what was actually agreed. And when something goes wrong on the day, there is no single source of truth to point to.

---

## What OiQb does

OiQb is a collaborative workspace for event agencies and their clients.

The agency builds a private venue library — uploading PDFs, floor plans, photos, and spec sheets. OiQb reads them and extracts the details that matter: capacity in every configuration, catering policy, AV specs, restrictions, contacts, pricing indicators. The library becomes structured and searchable.

When a client brief comes in, the planner picks venues from the library and generates an interactive pitch board — a private web page the client opens on any device. The client browses the options, asks questions, adjusts the spec, and approves. When they click Approve, the system locks a timestamped snapshot: an immutable record of exactly what was agreed.

The loop closes: brief in, approved spec out, everything sourced and traceable.

---

## Two layers

**Personal Venue Catalog** — the agency's private library. Documents go in, structured venue profiles come out. Searchable by capacity, catering policy, location, amenities. The data layer.

**Digital Sales Room** — the client-facing pitch board. Select venues, share a link, get sign-off. The output layer.

Neither works without the other. The catalog feeds the pitch. The pitch generates the approval.

---

## Who it is for

**Event agencies (5–50 people)** managing weddings, corporate events, conferences, and private parties. They already know the venues — they just need a faster way to turn that knowledge into a confirmed client brief.

**Independent event managers** who carry their venue knowledge in their head and their inbox, and need it somewhere searchable and shareable.

**Corporate event teams** that repeat the same venue research every event cycle because nothing was captured properly last time.

---

## How it works

1. Connect your Drive, Notion, or email — or upload files directly
2. OiQb extracts and structures the venue data automatically
3. Verify key fields with one click where the AI needs a check
4. Select venues for a brief, generate a pitch board, share a link
5. Client reviews, adjusts, and approves — one click, any device
6. The approved spec is locked with a timestamp and source citations

---

## How we make money

**$150/month per agency** — full library, full pitch board, unlimited team members. One approved deal pays for months.

**$300/month for larger teams** — priority processing, API access, white-label pitch boards.

**Future** — venues pay for a verified public profile that agencies can pull into pitches directly, without manual ingestion.

## One sentence

OiQb turns venue files into a pitch and a pitch into an approved spec — so event agencies close client briefs in hours, not days.
